# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Invintel.

## What we welcome now

- **Issues**: bug reports, questions, and ideas for what plants and warehouses need. Please describe the situation (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened) and leave out real customer or supplier data.
- **Security problems**: please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an issue.

## What we can't accept yet: code from outside

We are not taking pull requests from people outside the Bakerheit team yet. Before we can, we need a contributor sign-off in place (a Developer Certificate of Origin or a contributor agreement) so that everyone's right to submit their work is clear. Until then, please open an issue instead; if a pull request arrives we will thank you and close it without merging.

## Running it locally

See [README.md](README.md) (Getting started). In short: `pnpm install`, start MongoDB with `docker compose up -d`, then `pnpm dev`. Run `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` before proposing anything.

## Be decent

Be kind, be specific, assume good faith. We may close or lock threads that aren't.
